## The short version

Annie's is a dessert café with **two shops in Frisco, TX**. The original
is on FM 423 (opened August 2017). The second is at US-380 & Coit Rd
(opened May 2024). They sell macarons, cheesecakes, cakes, soft-baked
cookies, croffles, gelato, boba, smoothies and coffee. Customers rate
them well: 4.4★ and 4.6★ on Google, 4.6★ from 2,000+ DoorDash ratings.

Their website is a basic template from **Menufy**, their online-ordering
company. It's four thin pages on a tan background. The website doesn't
show hours, the owners' story, or a menu.

**Correction to what we thought:** they *do* have a full menu with prices.
It's just hidden. It only exists inside each store's separate ordering
page, the two stores have different menus and prices, and Google's "Menu"
link shows an old menu with years-old prices.

**The pitch:** one modern home page that looks like the shop (mint walls,
the red-hood mascot, the pink cupcake mural) and puts the real menu, both
stores' hours, cakes, gift boxes and birthday parties up front. Keep
Menufy for ordering (it works, they pay for it already); the new site
becomes the friendly front door.

## Why they need a new website

- **The menu is hidden.** No menu page on the website. Typing
  `/menu` just goes back to the homepage. The menu with prices only
  lives inside the ordering pages (fm423.anniessweetsntreats.com and
  380coit.anniessweetsntreats.com).
  Source: site capture of anniessweetsntreats.com, Oct 4.
- **Google shows an out-of-date menu.** The FM423 Google "Menu" link
  opens a SinglePlatform menu with old prices (medium Americano $2.45
  there; $2.95 on their own FM423 ordering page today, $3.99+ on the
  delivery apps). Source: http://places.singleplatform.com/annies-sweets--treats/menu?ref=google
- **The website doesn't show hours or addresses on the homepage.** Hours
  only appear on the ordering pages. Source: anniessweetsntreats.com.
- **Their hours are different on every listing.** Google, Yelp, Uber Eats,
  DoorDash and Tripadvisor all disagree (see "Not confirmed" below).
- **The FM423 phone number is wrong on Facebook and Tripadvisor.** They
  still show (214) 662-0165, the number from 2017. Sources:
  https://www.facebook.com/annies.sweetsntreats,
  https://communityimpact.com/frisco/news/annies-sweets-treats-opens-frisco-fm-423/
- **The FM423 address has three ZIP codes online** (75034, 75036, 75056),
  and DoorDash files it under "The Colony". Sources: website and Yelp
  (75034), Google (75036), DoorDash (75056).
- **The name is spelled at least five ways:** "Sweets & Treats" (logo),
  "Sweets and Treats" (page titles), "Sweets N Treats" (web address),
  "Sweet & Treats" (FM423 ordering page), "Sweets And Treat" (Uber Eats).
  Sources: anniessweetsntreats.com, https://www.ubereats.com/store/annies-sweets-and-treat-380-%26-coit/WkhmELlcX7eiKuid5Jl2NA
- **Custom-cake orders are where the worst reviews come from.** A prepaid
  $50 cheesecake wasn't ready at pickup (Jess S., Yelp, Jul 2025), and a
  pre-ordered cake was "not fresh" (Tiffany S., Yelp, Aug 2026). The
  ordering rules (1 day's notice for writing, 24-hour cancellation) are
  scattered across the ordering pages and DoorDash notes.
  Source: https://www.yelp.com/biz/annies-sweets-and-treats-frisco-2,
  https://www.yelp.com/biz/annies-sweets-and-treats-frisco
- **People can't find them.** "I'm shocked I never heard of this place
  before." (Jessica D., Yelp, Dec 2024). Yelp says people searched for
  desserts 4,901 times last month within 10 miles of the 380 & Coit shop.
  Source: https://www.yelp.com/biz/annies-sweets-and-treats-frisco-2
- **Visit Frisco featured them (Nov 2025) but couldn't link to a real
  site.** Source: https://visitfrisco.com/blog/holiday-eats-treats-frisco
- **The website has broken bits:** an empty "Contact Form" section, the
  Instagram link in the footer goes to an account that no longer exists,
  the Tripadvisor link goes to one person's review, the logo is tiny
  (360×300) and the tab icon is Menufy's, not theirs. Gallery photos are
  1–2 MB each (slow on phones). Source: site capture, Oct 4.
- **The 380 & Coit ordering page's description says "Plano"**, but the
  address is Frisco. Source: 380coit.anniessweetsntreats.com page code.

## What the concept site does differently

1. **The whole menu, on the website, for both shops**, with a switch
   between shops, tabs, a search box, a "gluten-free" filter and photos.
   Google can read it, so searches like "macarons Frisco" can find it.
2. **Open now / closed** for each shop, with tap-to-call and directions.
3. **Cakes & gift boxes in one place**, with the rules spelled out
   (8″ sizes, $5 writing, 1 day's notice, 24-hour cancellation).
4. **Birthday parties** with clear packages and one-tap email or call.
5. **Their best reviews up front**, with the real ratings.
6. **Looks like the shop**: mint, red and pink, the mascot, real photos,
   shrunk so the page loads fast on phones (1.7 MB for every photo
   together, vs. 25 MB on today's site).
7. **Clean, consistent details**: one spelling of the name, typos in the
   menu fixed ("Mathca", "caeamel", "Starwberry", "Lavendar"...), and
   working social links.
8. **Ordering stays on Menufy.** No change to how they take orders.
